Returns & Refunds

30-day money-back guarantee

If a piece is not right for you, contact us within 30 days of delivery for a return and full refund. No interrogation, no restocking fee.

How to return

  • Email us with your order number and a photo of the item.
  • We send return instructions and cover the return label for defective or wrongly-shipped items.
  • Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days after the return arrives.

Exceptions

  • Custom / personalized items (initial, name, handwriting pieces) cannot be returned unless defective — this is stated on each product page before checkout.
  • Earrings cannot be returned for hygiene reasons unless defective.

Damaged or wrong item?

Send us a photo within 7 days of delivery and we ship a replacement or refund immediately — you do not need to return the broken piece in most cases.
