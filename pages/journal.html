How to Care for Freshwater Pearls (So They Last Decades)

Freshwater pearls are organic gems: layers of nacre grown around a tissue nucleus. That nacre is beautiful and slightly porous, which is why pearl care is mostly about keeping harsh things away from it — not about babying your jewelry.

The one rule that matters most

Last on, first off. Put pearls on after makeup, perfume and hairspray, and take them off before showering or swimming. Alcohol and chlorine slowly dull nacre, and there is no way to polish it back.

Daily habits

  • Wipe each pearl with a soft, slightly damp cloth after wearing — skin oils are fine, cosmetics are not.
  • Store pearls flat and separate from metal chains, which can scratch nacre. The pouch in your Lueur box is made for this.
  • Wear them. Pearls keep their lustre better with occasional skin contact than sealed in a drawer.

What to avoid

Ultrasonic cleaners, jewellery dips and steam — all too aggressive for nacre. If a strand needs a deeper clean, lukewarm water with a drop of mild soap, then pat dry, is enough.

One reassuring note: small differences in shape, size or iridescence between pearls on the same piece are the signature of genuine freshwater pearls, not a defect.

Waterproof Gold vs. Regular Plating: What Actually Lasts

"Gold-filled", "18k plated", "waterproof" — the labels blur together, but underneath there is really one variable that decides how long a gold-tone piece survives daily life: the metal core, and how thick the gold layer over it is.

Why the core matters more than the coating

Fashion jewelry usually fails from the inside out. A brass or zinc-alloy core leaches through thin plating as the coating wears, turning the surface dull or green. Our waterproof pieces use a 316L stainless steel core — the same family of steel used in surgical tools — which does not leach, rust or discolour skin on its own.

What our finish actually promises

  • Thick 18k gold plating over the steel core, sealed with an anti-tarnish layer.
  • Shower-, sweat- and sea-safe for everyday wear.
  • Nickel-safe and hypoallergenic for most wearers.

Honest limits

No plating is invincible. Chlorine pools and hot-spring water are the two environments that will shorten any plated finish's life, so we recommend taking pieces off there. Abrasives — scouring powders, polishing cloths meant for raw metal — should stay away too.

If you want one decision rule: choose waterproof steel-core pieces for jewelry you never want to think about, and reserve delicate plating for occasional pieces.

A Practical Gift Guide: Personalized Jewelry, Chosen Well

Personalized jewelry tops wishlists for a reason — it reads as effort. But the difference between a piece that gets worn weekly and one that lives in the box is mostly choosing the right format for the person.

Match the format to the person

  • The minimalist: an initial letter necklace or a single engraved coordinate bar — quiet personal meaning, no visual noise.
  • The sentimental one: a handwriting bracelet engraved from a real note, or a custom name necklace in fine script.
  • The family builder: a family birthstone necklace with a lab-grown stone for each person they love, on one chain.
  • The stargazer: a zodiac constellation necklace drawn in cubic zirconia — meaning plus everyday sparkle.

Three checks before ordering custom

Confirm spelling and letter case in writing; choose chain length based on whether they wear chokers or longer layers; and check the personalization production time so the gift arrives for the date that matters.

Why lab-grown stones for birthstone gifts

Lab-grown gemstones are chemically and optically identical to mined ones at a friendlier price — which means a genuine birthstone gift is possible on almost any budget. Every Lueur gemstone piece is labeled lab-grown and ships with a disclosure card, because honest is the whole point.
