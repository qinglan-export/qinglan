Frequently asked questions

How long does shipping take?

Orders are dispatched within 1–3 business days after payment. Delivery estimates for your country are shown at checkout, and every order includes a tracking number.

Where do you ship from?

We ship worldwide via trusted fulfillment partners and tell you the dispatch origin for your destination at checkout.

What does "waterproof" actually mean?

Our waterproof pieces use a 316L stainless steel core with thick 18k gold plating and an anti-tarnish seal. Water, sweat and shower gel won't strip the finish. We still recommend keeping any plated jewelry out of chlorine pools and hot springs.

Are the pearls real?

Yes — genuine freshwater pearls, which is why no two are exactly the same size or shape. That irregularity is the look, not a defect.

What are "lab-grown" gemstones?

Gemstones grown in a laboratory rather than mined — chemically and optically identical to natural ones, at a fraction of the price. Every gemstone product is labeled lab-grown in the title and ships with a disclosure card. We never sell lab-grown stones as natural.

Is it safe for sensitive ears?

Earring posts are hypoallergenic steel; our 925 silver pieces use sterling posts. If you react to plated metals, choose pieces marked "925 silver" or the mother-of-pearl studs with surgical-steel posts.

Does it come gift-ready?

Every order ships in a protective box suitable for gifting as-is, and the price is never printed on the packing slip.

Do you ship worldwide?

We currently ship to the US, Canada, UK, and the EU. If your country is not listed at checkout, message us and we will check.
