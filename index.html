Frequently Asked Questions

What trade terms do you offer?

We only work on FOB terms, delivering goods to Chinese ports. CIF and DDP services are NOT available. The buyer will arrange ocean freight, insurance and destination clearance.

How much is your sourcing service fee?

Service cost depends on project complexity and order volume. We provide transparent quotation with no hidden charges after understanding your requirements.

Can you visit factories for inspection?

Yes. We can perform on-site factory audits and pre-shipment quality inspections to check product quality and production status.

What is the MOQ?

MOQ varies for different products. Some factories accept small trial orders, while bulk items require higher minimum quantity. We will confirm MOQ for each supplier.

Can you combine goods from multiple factories?

Yes, cargo consolidation is one of our core services. We collect goods from different suppliers and ship together under FOB.

Do you support OEM / ODM?

Yes, we can help you customize packaging, logo and product design according to your drawings or samples.
