What We Do

Full-Service Procurement Solutions

From product sourcing to door-to-door delivery, we handle every detail so you can scale your business with confidence.

Product Sourcing

Send us your product spec, reference photo or target price. We identify and shortlist 3–5 matched factories within 3 working days.

Supplier Verification

We audit factory credentials, production capacity, certifications and trade history. You only get vetted, reliable manufacturers.

Quality Control

Pre-production, during-production and pre-shipment inspections. Photo/video reports before goods leave the factory.

Shipping & Logistics

Air, sea and express freight from Shenzhen/Guangzhou. FOB, CIF, DDP terms available. We handle customs and documentation.

Consolidation

Buy from multiple factories? We combine your orders into one shipment, saving you freight costs and simplifying customs.

OEM / Private Label

Custom packaging, logo printing, product modification and branding. We manage the entire customization process.
